# The optical colour model of the glass against real images (section 12)

Real: 4079 colour pairs of 29 TRansPose sequences. Simulator: 3000 paired windows of `V2_place_opt` (the same window with and without the glass); for comparison the alpha model, `simpair`.

## 1. The simulator's own network, out of fold

| state | pairs | AUC (95%) |
|---|---|---|
| shows | 1413 | 1.00 (1.00 to 1.00) |
| faint | 425 | 0.92 (0.90 to 0.93) |
| blind | 1162 | 0.50 (0.50 to 0.50) |

Floor (rule 1): 0.50 in the colour-blind pairs: within 0.45 to 0.55.
